Cancellation Policy

Skill Boost Nexus provides digital academy access plans that are activated online. This policy explains how plan cancellations and downgrades are handled.

Plan Cancellation

Academy owners can stop using a paid plan by switching to another available plan from the owner dashboard, including the Basic plan when available.

When Cancellation Takes Effect

Plan changes made from the dashboard take effect on the platform once the switch is successfully applied. Access limits, instructor seats, and student seats are then updated based on the newly active plan.

Previously Purchased Plans

If a paid plan was already purchased for the same academy and remains available in the account history, the academy may be allowed to switch back to that plan without completing the checkout flow again, subject to the current platform rules in effect.

Need Help

If you need help canceling or changing a plan, contact us at skillbooost.academy@gmail.com.
