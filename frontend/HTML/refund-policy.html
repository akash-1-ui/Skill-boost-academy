Refund Policy

Skill Boost Nexus sells digital academy access plans. Because plan benefits are activated online, refunds are reviewed case by case.

General Rule

Payments made for successfully activated digital access plans are generally non-refundable once the plan has been activated on the academy account.

When a Refund May Be Considered

A refund request may be reviewed if there was a duplicate charge, a technical error that charged the customer without activating the selected plan, or any other billing issue verified by our support team.

How To Request a Refund

Please email skillbooost.academy@gmail.com with your academy name, registered email address, payment reference, and a short description of the issue so we can review the request.

Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, the amount will be processed back through the original payment channel based on the timelines followed by Razorpay and the relevant bank or payment provider.
